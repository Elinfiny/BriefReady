# BriefReady

A browser-local delivery worksheet for freelancers turning a brief into a checkable handoff.

## The Unique Kernel
Every deliverable keeps its original brief excerpt beside editable acceptance checks. Suggested checks are visibly distinguished from client requirements, so vague scope is not quietly turned into a promise.

## Who It's For
A solo freelancer preparing a small design, writing or website handoff from a text brief.

## The Core Loop
Paste a brief, generate a local draft, review source-linked deliverables, edit and reorder them, check agreed acceptance criteria, package each item, and export the worksheet. Reopen a saved JSON worksheet without a service or account.

## Inspiration & Identity
Executor-proposed private direction: a calm editorial workbench, warm paper, forest green, visible numbering and generous writing space. No external assets, fonts or brand references.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Personal motivation was not collected. Freelancer usefulness is the admitted target, not an invented testimonial.

## What "Working" Looks Like
For a synthetic two-deliverable brief, the app shows two source-linked cards, recognizes explicitly prefixed acceptance lines, highlights vague wording, preserves edits locally, exports Markdown/JSON and restores the same checklist from JSON. Nothing is sent to a service.

## The POC Boundary
One worksheet, one screen, deterministic draft suggestions, editable deliverables/criteria, reorder and remove, local save, JSON round-trip, Markdown handoff.

## Later
User research and clearer language handling, after evidence from the small loop.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts, collaboration, backend, runtime model calls, paid APIs, invoices and payments: unnecessary to prove this loop. No reuse of code from other portfolio projects.
